## One exchange, carried both ways

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Out to the room">
    The board's event arrives from the suite's event relay. SuperExchange makes the board's room
    once, invites its people, waits for one of them to join, and posts the card exactly once,
    encrypted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Back to the board">
    A person answers in the room. Accounts says who they are; only a person may answer. The
    answer acts at SuperPipeline as that person, and one receipt is posted.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nothing lost between">
    A sweep asks every opted-in board what it still waits on: cards the relay missed are posted,
    and a gate decided on the web or in `supi` is settled in chat with the same one receipt.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

SuperExchange is early: v0.0.2 runs in production, and no production board has moved to it yet.
[Status and limits](/status/) says exactly what works and what is still owed by other products.
