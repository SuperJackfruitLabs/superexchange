## One exchange, carried both ways

Here is the whole trip in four stops. We carry the work out, bring the answer back, and keep the
room and board in step without asking either product to surrender its own job.

**Out to the room.** The board's event arrives from the suite's event relay. SuperExchange makes the
board's room once, invites its people, waits for one of them to join, and posts the card exactly
once, encrypted. You get one readable card instead of a relay retry collection. See
[Board rooms](/board-chat/rooms/).

**Back to the board.** You answer in the room. Accounts says who you are; only a person may answer.
The answer acts at SuperPipeline as you, and one receipt is posted. The room now shows the same
decision the board recorded. See
[Gates](/board-chat/gates/).

**Nothing lost between.** A sweep asks every opted-in board what it still waits on: cards the relay
missed are posted, and a gate decided on the web or in `supi` is settled in chat with the same one
receipt. The fast path can miss a beat without making the room invent a different ending. See
[The sweeps](/board-chat/sweeps/).

**Nothing requires it.** SuperPipeline stays a board without it, and its gates are still decided on
the web and in `supi`. People read and answer in any Matrix client. See
[What SuperExchange is](/what-it-is/).
